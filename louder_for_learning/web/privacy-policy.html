Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 20, 2026

Louder for Learning ("the App") helps K–12 teachers at underfunded schools discover and apply for educational grants. This policy explains what information the App collects, why, and how it's protected.

Information We Collect

When you create an account and use the App, we collect:

  • Account information: your email address (via Firebase Authentication), used to sign you in and verify your account.
  • Teacher profile: your name, school, school level, subjects taught, and Title I / underfunded-school status, which you provide during onboarding to help match you with relevant grants.
  • Grant applications: the project name, requested funding amount, project description, and any documents you upload when you apply for a grant through the App, along with the status of each application.

We do not collect payment information, location data, or contacts. The App does not show ads and does not use analytics tracking.

How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and keep your account secure.
  • To show you grants relevant to your school and subjects.
  • To submit and track the status of the grant applications you choose to submit.

We do not sell your information or share it with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored using Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore). Access to your profile and application data is restricted to your account only, enforced by server-side security rules — no other user of the App can read or write your data.

Your Rights

You can review and update your profile at any time from the "Edit Profile" screen in the App. You can permanently delete your account and all associated data — your profile and every grant application you've submitted — at any time from My Account → Delete Account. This action is immediate and cannot be undone.

Data Retention

We retain your account and application data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your profile and application records are permanently deleted from our systems.

Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by teachers and school staff, not by children. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
